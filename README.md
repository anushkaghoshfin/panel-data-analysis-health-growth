# Panel Data Analysis of Health Indicators and Economic Growth

## Objective

To examine the relationship between health indicators and GDP per capita of 
selected South Asian and Southeast Asian countries using panel data analysis.

## data structure
13 countries x 21 years (2000-2020)

## Selected South Asian and Southeast Asian economies
The countries included in the study are India, Pakistan,Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, the Philippines, Cambodia, and Brunei Darussalam

## varibles
● Gross Domestic Product per capita (GDPPC) is used as the dependent variable 
representing economic growth. 
● Life expectancy at birth (LEB) and under-5 mortality rate (U5MR) are used as 
indicators of health outcomes. 
● Current health expenditure per capita (CHEPC) and domestic private health 
expenditure per capita (DPHE) are included to represent healthcare spending.  
● General government expenditure (GGE) as a percentage of GDP is used as a control 
variable. 

## model 
LGDPPCit = β0 + β1LLEBit + β2LU5MRit +β3LCHEPCit +β4LDPHEit + β5LGGEit + uit
